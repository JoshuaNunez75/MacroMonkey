MacroMonkey

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

MacroMonkey is a calorie and macronutrient tracking app built and operated by an independent developer. This policy explains exactly what the app collects, where it goes, and how to get rid of it.

There are no advertisements in MacroMonkey, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and your information is never sold, rented, or shared with data brokers or advertisers.

Information the app collects

Account information

Creating an account requires an email address and a password. Accounts are handled by Google Firebase Authentication. Your password is stored by Firebase in hashed form and is never visible to the developer.

Information you enter

  • Your goals and body details — calorie target, macronutrient split, and, if you use the built-in calculator, your age, sex, height, weight, activity level, and weekly weight-change goal.
  • Your food log — the foods you record, the serving and quantity, which meal you assigned them to, the time, and the date they count toward.

Technical information

Google's infrastructure records standard operational data such as IP addresses, timestamps, and basic device information, in order to run the service and protect it from abuse. The app itself does not collect device identifiers or build any advertising profile.

What the app does not collect

  • No location data.
  • No contacts, photos, or files.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no behavioural tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No payment information — MacroMonkey does not charge for anything.

Camera and barcode scanning

MacroMonkey asks for camera permission for one purpose: scanning barcodes on food packaging.

The camera feed is processed on your device to read the barcode. No photo or video is ever saved, uploaded, or transmitted. The only thing that leaves your phone is the decoded barcode number, which is used to look up the product's nutrition information.

Who else receives information

Google Firebase

MacroMonkey uses Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Functions to run accounts, store your data, and handle food lookups. Your account and log data are stored on Google's servers as part of providing the app. Google acts as the infrastructure provider and does not use this data for its own advertising purposes.

FatSecret

Nutrition information comes from the FatSecret Platform API. When you search for a food or scan a barcode, the search text or barcode number is sent to FatSecret through the app's own server.

FatSecret does not receive your identity. Your email address, your account, your goals, and your food log are never sent to them — only the words you searched for.

Nobody else

Information is disclosed to no other parties, except where required by law.

Developer access

As the operator of the service, the developer has administrative access to the database and could technically view stored account and log data. This access is used only for maintaining and troubleshooting the app, and passwords are not accessible to anyone, including the developer.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your information is kept for as long as your account exists.

You can delete your account at any time from inside the app: open Settings, tap "Delete account", and confirm with your password. This permanently removes your account, your goals, and every food entry you have logged. It cannot be undone.

You can also email macromonkey75@gmail.com to request deletion, or to ask what information is held about you.

Security

Connections between the app and its servers are encrypted in transit. Database access rules are enforced on the server so that a signed-in user can read and write only their own data. No system is perfectly secure, and no guarantee of absolute security can be made.

Children

MacroMonkey is not directed at children under 13, and information is not knowingly collected from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email the address below and it will be deleted.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new "last updated" date. Material changes affecting how your information is handled will be communicated in the app.
